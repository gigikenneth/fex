About This Project

FemTech Africa Database

This interactive dashboard maps the growing ecosystem of female-focused health technology (FemTech) startups across Africa.

FemTech encompasses technology solutions tailored to women's health needs including maternal care, reproductive health, menstrual health, fertility, breast cancer screening, and general women's wellness.

Key Findings
  • Africa has a rapidly growing femtech ecosystem with startups across all regions
  • West Africa (particularly Nigeria) and East Africa (particularly Kenya) lead in innovation
  • ~81% of funded African femtech startups focus on fertility and maternal health
  • Significant gaps remain in menopause, pelvic health, and endometriosis solutions
  • Mobile and SMS-based platforms dominate, reflecting Africa's mobile-first landscape

Last updated: April 2026
